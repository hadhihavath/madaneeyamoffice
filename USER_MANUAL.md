## 1. Logging In & Account Security

### How to Access the Portal
1. Open your web browser on your smartphone, tablet, or laptop.
2. Navigate to your organization’s portal URL:
   ```text
   https://your-domain.com/login
   ```
3. Enter your official **Work Email Address** (e.g. `yourname@ceem.edu` or your registered email).
4. Enter your **Password**.
5. Click **"Sign In to Workspace"**.

### Security Guidelines
* **Keep your credentials confidential**: Never share your login details with colleagues or third parties.
* **Always Sign Out** when using shared office computers or front desk terminals.
* If you suspect your password has been compromised, contact your **Office Manager** or **HR Department** immediately for a password reset.

## 2. Geofenced Daily Attendance

Our office portal uses smart GPS geofencing to accurately record staff arrival and departure times while eliminating paperwork.

### Step-by-Step: Clocking In
1. When you arrive at your assigned office branch, go to **Attendance** from the navigation menu.
2. Ensure your phone or device’s **GPS / Location Services** are enabled.
3. Click the green **"Check In"** button.
4. The system will verify that your GPS coordinates fall within the allowed office boundary (e.g., within 100–150 meters of the branch).
5. Once verified:
   - A confirmation banner will indicate **"Checked In Successfully"**.
   - Your live shift timer will begin recording your workday.

### Step-by-Step: Clocking Out
1. At the conclusion of your shift, return to the **Attendance** page.
2. Click the red **"Check Out"** button.
3. The system will finalize your daily working hours and record your total shift duration.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Anti-Fraud & Accuracy Rules:**
> - You cannot check in multiple times in a single day without checking out.
> - The system automatically flags simulated locations or impossible coordinate jumps.
> - Check-ins attempted outside the configured office radius will be rejected.

## 3. Mobile & Browser GPS Setup

To check in successfully, your browser must be allowed to read your GPS location.

### On Android (Google Chrome)
1. When prompted with *"ceem-portal wants to know your location"*, tap **Allow**.
2. If location was previously blocked:
   - Tap the **Padlock icon (🔒)** or **Tune icon** in Chrome's address bar.
   - Tap **Permissions** ➔ **Location** ➔ Select **Allow**.
   - Ensure your phone's general **Location / GPS** toggle is turned ON with "High Accuracy" enabled.

### On iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)
1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
2. Scroll down and tap **Privacy & Security** ➔ **Location Services** (Ensure it is **ON**).
3. Scroll down to **Safari Websites** and select **While Using the App**.
4. In Safari, refresh the portal page and tap **Allow** when prompted for location access.

### On Desktop / Laptop (Windows & Mac)
* For best accuracy on desktop computers, ensure your device is connected to the office **Wi-Fi network**. Wi-Fi triangulation allows your laptop browser to locate the office accurately.

## 4. Leave Management System

Employees can request planned or emergency leaves directly through the portal without manual forms.

### Submitting a Leave Request
1. Go to the **Leave** section from the sidebar or bottom menu.
2. Review your **Remaining Leave Balance** (Casual, Sick, Emergency).
3. Click **"Request Leave"**.
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Leave Type**: Select *Casual Leave*, *Sick Leave*, or *Emergency Leave*.
   - **Start Date & End Date**.
   - **Reason**: Provide a brief justification for the absence.
5. Click **"Submit Application"**.

### Tracking Your Application Status
* **Pending**: Your application has been routed to your Office Manager or HR for review.
* **Approved**: The leave is granted, and your attendance record is marked accordingly.
* **Rejected**: The request was declined. You can view the manager's review remarks directly in the leave history table.

## 8. FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Q1: The portal says "You are 180 meters away from the office. Check-in rejected." What should I do?
* **Solution**: Ensure you are inside the building or within the campus boundary. Refresh your browser while standing near an open window or connecting to the official office Wi-Fi, then click **Check In** again.

### Q2: I forgot to check out at the end of yesterday's shift. How do I fix it?
* **Solution**: Your shift will automatically register the end of the day or stay open for manual adjustment. Contact your **Office Manager** or **HR Admin** to log an attendance correction.

### Q3: Why is my browser saying "Location access denied"?
* **Solution**: Your browser or operating system has blocked location permissions. Follow the steps in [Section 3: Mobile & Browser GPS Setup](#3-mobile--browser-gps-setup) to reset permissions to **Allow**.

### Q4: Can I submit a leave request after taking an emergency medical leave?
* **Solution**: Yes. Submit a **Sick Leave** application as soon as you return, and note the medical emergency in the reason box.
